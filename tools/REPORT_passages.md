## Manual QA (2026-09-26, round-3 rules build)

- **Source and build.** The 60 passages are hand-written for this pack
  (`tools/passages_src.json`, japanese 6c475c2, two QA rounds). A1 and A2 use
  です/ます throughout, including notices. Plain form appears only in B1
  narration, B1 articles and quoted speech, and in the B1 friends' chat
  (p0049). The questions sort into reading order, and each mc key cycles
  through positions 0-3. Every declared name is also in `oop` with a reason
  starting "name". Two runs give byte-identical `pack/passages.json`.
- **Builder.** vocab-engine packbuilder with the Japanese passage hooks in
  `langs/ja.py` (branch ja-passage-rules). The word and sentence build never
  call these hooks.
  - Grammar tokens outside the pack are not counted when they link nothing.
    This covers ん, れる/られる, せる, う, たり, けど, って, な, なら and the
    compound particles (について, にとって). It also covers a verb or adjective
    in auxiliary use after て (ている, てしまう, てくる, てほしい) and する after
    its noun (勉強する).
  - The length band counts the tokens the coverage counts. Particles the pack
    has (は, が, を, に, で, と, も, の, か, よ, ね, て, から, まで) are counted.
  - Classify gets a lemma alias from the pack's alt spellings. An alt that
    belongs to one word and is no headword links that word, so kana みんな
    links 皆 and kanji 所 links ところ. ごろ after a time word links 頃.
  - A declared name is one PROPN token and never a pack word. あかり is not
    明かり, and a name Sudachi splits is joined.
  - Passage tagging groups words over the corpus plus the passages, as the
    build does. Passage tagging never saves the groups file.
- **Round-3 linking rules (passage-only, `langs/ja.py`).** Each fixes a
  class, not a passage. Counts are spans in `pack/passages.json` before and
  after (wrong / occurrences).

  | residual | rule | before | after |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 欲しい inside てほしい | ほしい after て/で is grammar, not counted | 5/5 | 0/5 |
  | quotative という / っていう | kana いう after と/って before a noun is grammar; だという (hearsay) and と言う人 keep 言う | 5/5 | 0/5 |
  | 前 "ago" | after a time amount 前 is the noun 前 "before, ago", に the particle | 4/8 | 0/8 |
  | 3点 | a numeral + 点 is the counter 〜点 (unchanged) | 0/4 | 0/4 |
  | 後 read ご | その後 is joined; 1ヶ月後 is a suffix, not 後 あと | 3/3 | 0/3 |
  | 一つ, 三つ | numeral + つ is one span 〜つ, not 一 いち + つ | 5/5 | 0/5 |
  | compound fragments | 一緒に, 急に, 前に, 先に: one span over X + に; その後, 一番 joined | 25 | 0 |
  | ただ、 (conjunction) | links the adverb ただ "only", not ただ "ordinary" | 4/5 | 0/5 |
  | たった | out of pack, never ただ (no use in the texts) | 0/0 | 0/0 |
  | どれ位, 三日位 | 位 read くらい is くらい, not 位 "rank" (no use in the texts) | 0/0 | 0/0 |

  The ただ gloss has no "but, however" sense; the conjunction links the
  adverb as the nearest sense.
- **Homographs checked over every span.** いつ links いつ, not 何時. Four
  uses of 方 all read ほう, "side, comparison", and 会社の方へ was rewritten.
  人 splits into ひと and the 〜人 counter by position. 日 is ひ and 〜日. 時
  splits into とき and 〜時. 分 is only 〜分 "minutes". 開く is always ひらく.
  撮る and 取る stay apart, and so do 暑い and 熱い. 高い covers both "tall" and
  "expensive", which is one pack word. The contraction 並んでる, read by
  Sudachi as 出る, was rewritten as 並んでいる. Declared names link no pack word.
- **Questions.** The set has 297 questions: A1 98, A2 99, B1 100. Every
  question's `words` are linked in its sentence. The correct mc option is the
  unique longest in 25 of 177 items: A1 4, A2 10, B1 11. Correct mc answers
  sit at positions 0/1/2/3 as 45/44/44/44. True/false answers are 60 true and
  60 false. No true/false item shares a run of more than 9 characters with its
  passage, apart from p0013, which repeats the time 3時間くらいでした。(10
  with the full stop). Two A2 questions answerable from general knowledge were
  replaced with passage details: when cherry trees bloom (p0033) and what to
  do first in an earthquake (p0030).
- **Out-of-pack lemmas.** 印刷 "print" (p0029), 揺れ "shaking" (p0030),
  花見 (the topic word of p0033), 充電 "battery charge" (p0037), and 鍋 "pot"
  and 煮る "simmer" in the soup recipe (p0044). None has a pack paraphrase.
  Words the pack lacks were written around rather than declared. Examples are
  毎朝, 次の日, 映画館, 入口, 半年, 引っ越す and おめでとう.
- **Known limits.** お茶 splits into お and 茶, which links the B1 word 茶, so
  A1/A2 avoid お茶. The honorific prefix お/ご is an unlinked miss wherever it
  remains (p0022 お待ち). 何曜日, 何人, 何歳, 何階 and 何時ごろ are single
  Sudachi tokens outside the pack, so the questions avoid them.
- **Generated files.** `pack/sentences.js` is stale until
  `tools/jsonify_pack.py pack` runs. `validate_pack.py` reports only that.
- **Groups-cache clobber (a defect class, fixed in the engine).**
  Linker.pretag re-tags passages through the spec's tagger. Japanese.tag_texts
  used to rebuild its word groups from those few hundred texts and save them
  over the corpus file `.cache/derived/ja_groups_*.json.gz`, which broke the
  next context rebuild. The engine now skips that save while the spec tags
  passages, and a tripwire in Linker.pretag fails on any derived-cache write.
  This build left `ja_groups_t22_fe4f59144e.json.gz` unchanged.
